# WO-0029 Skimmed: spec notes (second spec)

Passed with no blocking fault. Both faults of the inspection send-back are fixed: Apply now takes a new `of` with its text or within 500ms after it and credits neither report (walked through in both orders, A4 and A6 cover both), and `all shown` is drawn only when every counted caveat was judged, with a drawn "not known" state for doubt and for a reply never in the window. Every hook and call named is in the types file (2.1.289). 119 lines, 15 acceptance lines. Settle these while building; each is looked at again at inspection.

1. The module-level `const queue` is a declared departure from the standard. Keep it exactly as narrow as the spec says: reports only, emptied by every flush, by `clear` and by off, never read by a render. Anything more held at module level is a fault.
2. Apply step 1 asks that the report "has the page's `of`", but a report whose `on` is null carries no `of`. Read a null report as carrying the page's own `of`, so the rows of `page.on` are paid for the time up to the moment the window left. Otherwise a person who scrolls back, reads for two seconds and scrolls away earns nothing and the caveat stays quoted.
3. In the settle order the first report of a step sets `page.on` to the old window under the new text. That is harmless only because credit needs an equal `of` and the 500ms rule holds. The A4 test in that order must check `ms` is 0 on every row at `turn.complete`, not just the card.
4. A4's first report has the caveat on line 32 inside the window (rows 0 to 39 of 40). The test must prove it earns nothing there because the text is still changing, with real growing texts, not by starting the window past it.
5. `1 earlier in show` is 17 characters and the inner width at 20 columns is 16. Give it a short form under 30 (`<k> earlier`), as the button and the `more` row have, and cover it in A14. `Nothing to quote from the last turn.` must wrap, not cut, at 20.
6. A turn that has both off-screen caveats and unjudged ones draws the quotes and says nothing of the unjudged. Add the dim `<u> not judged` row under the quotes when `u` is 1 or more, so the count in the note is not read as the whole truth.
7. Which card wins when two states hold at once is still not stated: a turn running with no page (empty or working), a turn running with no page outside fullscreen (error or working), pages held with only turn 0 and nothing busy. Suggested order: error, then working, then empty, then at rest; with only turn-0 pages, the empty sentence.
8. "Its place is the middle row": take the middle of the caveat's own rows before widening and clipping. That gives the 20% and 55% of A4.
9. The good-news rule ("one of the two words before it is `0`, `no`, `none` or `nothing`") must not swallow `No, I did not run the migration.` Compare whole words inside the same clause: a word followed by a comma, colon or semicolon does not count. Add that sentence to the A8 data as one that is found.
10. A10 says "with the text whole": the text is the stored one, cut at 200 characters with `…`. `show` cannot print more, since the reply's text is never kept.
11. `clear` during a running turn keeps `isBusy`, so the card shows the working sentence after `Skimmed cleared.` A12's "returns to the empty sentence" holds at rest; test it at rest.
12. The `isOn` read is the only state read in the `AssistantMessage` hook, and the hook's `try` covers `sync($)` as well as the push.
13. The live run cannot prove the signal. At inspection the headless run is judged on what it can show (reply word for word, the error sentences from `show`, store holding `isOn` only). The four-part fullscreen hand check stays the owner's to make; the builder must not run it.
