# Seen (`seen-widget`)
## Purpose
For anyone who lets Claude judge from a picture: a screenshot of the page it built, a plot, a design file. The terminal says an image was read and nothing more, so "the page renders correctly" is taken on faith. Seen draws the newest picture Claude received on the card, lists the three before it, and `open` puts the full picture in the person's browser, so they check the evidence instead of the sentence. No model call. Kept from the idea: every picture from `Read` and from MCP results, drawn as Claude received it, newest large with a numbered list, `open`, `clear`, the `Image` element on terminals that draw pixels, a real thumbnail everywhere else. Changed, with the reason: the bytes Claude received are kept in session state for file pictures too, and drawing and `open` use them, never the path. A screenshot path is routinely overwritten by the next run, and a card that showed the new file beside the old claim would be false evidence. Cut: the turn number (the time of day is shown instead and needs no extra hook), a view switch (the terminal is told from the environment), a decoder for JPEG, GIF and WebP, a grid of thumbnails, anything kept across sessions.
Stated plainly, as the idea asked: `ImageSource` takes PNG or raw pixels only, so a JPEG, GIF or WebP picture has no preview on any terminal; it is a row of text and `open` shows it. PNG is what `Read` returns for a PNG file within its limits (the types give no re-encoding rule; the live run records the type) and what Playwright and chrome-devtools screenshots default to; computer-use style screenshots are often JPEG. If the live run shows `Read` handing back JPEG for an ordinary PNG file, send the order back to ideation.
## Terms
- A picture: from a `Read` call whose result is `{ type: 'image', file: { base64, type } }`, source `e.file_path`; or from a tool named `mcp__*` (its result is typed `unknown`, so it is walked defensively): the block list is the result when it is an array, else its `content` when that is an array; a block counts when `type === 'image'` and it carries string `data` with `mimeType`, or `source.data` with `source.media_type`. Source is the tool name after the second `__`. At most 4 pictures per call, the last ones. A result with `deny` or `isError` gives none. Pictures from a subagent's loop (`e.agentId` set) count and are marked `agent`. A source is always a string: anything else (a `Read` call with no `file_path`) is `(no path)`, and each run of control characters, line breaks included, becomes one space.
- Type: `PNG`, `JPEG`, `GIF` or `WEBP` from the declared media type; any other media type is not a picture. Size: decoded bytes from the base64 length, written `<n> B` under 1000, whole `KB` under 1000 KB, else `MB` with one decimal. Width and height: from the PNG header; for other types from `file.dimensions` display then original values when `Read` gives them, else unknown and not drawn.
- Number: the first picture of the session is `#1`, each later one the newest number plus one. The card holds the newest 4.
- Thumb: never made in the `tool.call` hook. Measured in `claude plugin test` on 2.1.289 the decode costs about 2.1 microseconds a pixel there (1280×720 2.0 to 2.5 s, 1920×1080 4.2 to 5.2 s), all of it the hook's own time under `HookBudget.ms`, 10 000. So the hook only lists the picture, and a job on a timer draws the thumb afterwards in slices. Eligible: a kept, 8-bit, non-interlaced PNG of colour type 0, 2, 3, 4 or 6, at most 4 200 000 pixels (2560×1600 fits) and neither side over 4096. Such a shot is listed with `isPending` true; every other picture has no thumb and is never pending. Only the newest shot is ever pending: a new picture clears the mark on the ones before it, which are drawn as one row and need no thumb.
- Job: `sync` cancels any running timer; then, when the widget is on and the newest shot is pending, it reads that shot's slot, starts a generator over the bytes and sets `timer = $.clock.every(5, step)`. The generator is the pure inflate (stored, fixed and dynamic blocks; the module has no `DecompressionStream` and no `WebAssembly`) feeding rows as they come, with no whole-picture buffer: the five row filters undone, alpha laid over white, box-averaged to fit 224 by 48 thumb pixels where a thumb pixel counts twice as tall as wide. One `step` takes one slice, `max(1, floor(16384 / W))` rows (about 35 ms at the measured rate); the step that takes the last row cancels the timer and writes the thumb and `isPending` false to the shot with the job's id, if it is still held. A stream that fails or ends short does the same with no thumb. Expected from the measured rate: 60 slices and about 2.5 s to the preview for 1280×720, 135 and about 5.5 s for 1920×1080, about 11 s at the ceiling. `sync` runs at `session.start`, after every change of the switch, after a capture that listed a picture, and after `clear`; a reload therefore starts a pending thumb again from the kept bytes.
- Box: inner width is the card's less 4. `rows = round(inner * H / W / 2)`, `columns = inner`; when rows is over 12, `rows = 12` and `columns = max(1, round(24 * W / H))`; rows is at least 1.
- Pixel terminal: surface `terminal`, `TMUX` unset, and `KITTY_WINDOW_ID` set or `TERM` holding `kitty` or `TERM_PROGRAM` equal to `ghostty`. Read when a picture is captured.
- Kept: the base64 as received, when it is at most 4 000 000 characters (one `$.fs.write` takes 4 MiB, and `$.state.set` refuses any one value over 4 194 304 characters of JSON, measured on 2.1.289). Each picture's bytes are a state value of their own, in slot `String(id % 4)` of the `kept` family, so no value ever holds two pictures and a new picture overwrites the slot of the one it drops. A larger picture is listed but not kept and its slot is set to `null`. The slot is written before `shots`; if that write rejects, the picture is listed as not kept. A slot is believed only when its `id` equals the shot's.
- Page: `<$.plugin.root>/seen.html`, one file, written only by `open`: a doctype, a `<title>` of `Seen #<n> <source>` with `&`, `<`, `>` and `"` escaped, and one `<img src="data:<media type>;base64,<data>">`. Emptied means overwritten with an empty string (`$.fs` has no remove).
## Card
Title `Seen`. Note: `plural(n, 'picture')` where n is the newest number; none when empty. Newest picture: a head row `#<n> <source>` with `<W>×<H>` at the right (a path is cut from its start with `…`, the fact stays whole); the picture in its box; a dim detail row `<HH:MM> · <TYPE> · <size>` plus ` · agent` for a subagent's; a dim hint row `/seen-widget open for full size`. Then up to 3 earlier pictures, one dim row each: `#<n> <source>` with the type at the right. Under 30 columns: no `<W>×<H>`, no type fact, no hint row. The picture is, first that fits: an `Image` (pixel terminal, PNG, at most 2 MiB decoded); the thumb through `$.widgets.picture`, averaged down to twice the box in each direction; else one dim row: `Drawing the preview…` while pending (`Drawing…` under 30 columns), `Not kept: too large.` when not kept, otherwise `No preview for <TYPE> here.`, which is also what a PNG past the ceiling shows; `open` works for it all the same.

## Commands
`/seen-widget [on|off|show|open [n]|clear]`, the verb matched without regard to case. Bare, `on`, `off` and the usage as in the template (`Seen on; /widgets places it.`, `Seen off.`). No second command, no tool.
- `show`: one line per held picture, newest first: `#<n> <tool> <full source>: <TYPE> <W>×<H>, <size>, <HH:MM>, <preview|preview coming|no preview>`, the tool being `Read` or the full MCP tool name, the size part without `<W>×<H>` when unknown, with ` (agent)` and ` (not kept)` appended where true. `No pictures yet.` when empty.
- `open` opens the newest, `open <n>` that number. It writes the page, then starts a viewer with `$.process.run`, first that resolves with exit code 0: when env `OS` is `Windows_NT`, `['rundll32', 'url.dll,FileProtocolHandler', page]`; otherwise `['open', page]`, then `['xdg-open', page]`. Answers `Opened #<n>: <page>`; when no viewer starts, `Could not start a viewer. #<n> is at <page>`. Empty list: `No pictures yet.` A number not held: `No picture <n>. The card holds #<a> to #<b>.` Not kept: `#<n> was too large to keep (<size>).` and nothing is written. An `n` that is not a whole number above 0, or has more than 9 digits, answers the usage. The page path is `$.plugin.root`, then `\` when the root holds a `\` and `/` otherwise, then `seen.html`.
- `clear`: empties the list and the kept bytes, empties the page if this session wrote it, restarts the numbering; answers `Seen cleared.`
- While off, `show`, `open` and `clear` answer `Seen is off.` and change nothing.
## Data
Verified in this build's types (`plugin-authoring/types/claude-code.d.ts`, 2.1.289; `plugins/*/.claude-plugin/types/` is absent from this checkout).
- `on('tool.call', hook)`, no matcher, once per tool call. `ToolCallInput` carries `tool`, `tool_use_id`, the arguments (`file_path` for `Read`) and `agentId` ("absent on the main loop"). Order: off, `return next(e)`. `const ran = await next(e)`; unless `e.tool` is `Read` or starts with `mcp__`, return `ran`. The capture (the PNG header, the state writes, `sync`) runs inside `try`/`catch` and decodes no pixel, and the hook always returns `ran` itself, so `ref` stands and Claude reads what it would have read. `BuiltinToolResults['Read']` image variant: `{ type: "image", file: { base64, type: "image/jpeg" | "image/png" | "image/gif" | "image/webp", originalSize, dimensions?: { originalWidth?, originalHeight?, displayWidth?, displayHeight? } } }`. `ToolResultOf` is `unknown` for an MCP tool; `McpContentBlock` (`type`, `mimeType`, loose fields) is the shape `$.mcp.call` returns and is the first shape tried.
- `Image` from `$.ui.resolve(e)`, in `Elements['terminal']` only ("pixels over a box of cells where the terminal can (kitty, Ghostty), the `alt` elsewhere"). `ImageProps`: `key?`, `source`, `columns`, `rows` (1 to 255), `alt` (required). `ImageSource` `{ png: string }`, "a whole PNG file, base64", "at most 2 MiB decoded". Drawn as `<Image key="seen" source={{ png }} columns rows alt="#<n> <source>" />`. `$.ui.blit` is not used: a new picture is a state write and a redraw.
- `$.widgets.picture({ surface, key, columns, rows, marks })`: one `[x, y, 0xRRGGBB]` mark per picture pixel, `columns` and `rows` twice the box.
- `$.clock.every(ms, fn)` (`TimerCall = (ms: number, fn: () => void) => Timer`; "One `clock.every` dispatch per period: `fn` runs when it resolves and the next period is asked") and `Timer.cancel()` ("a stopped timer never fires again"). `HookBudget`: "Each bounds the hook's OWN time: the clock stops while a `next(e)` call or any `$` call of the hook's is in flight".
- `$.env.get(name)` ("`name` must be a string literal") for `TMUX`, `KITTY_WINDOW_ID`, `TERM`, `TERM_PROGRAM` at capture and `OS` at `open`.
- `$.fs.write(path: string, text: string)` (text only, which is why the page is HTML with a data URI and not an image file), `$.plugin.root`, `$.process.run(argv, { timeoutMs: 5000 })` ("Rejects when the command cannot start"), `$.clock.now()` once per capture.
- `on('session.end')`: while on and the page was written, empties it, then `next(e)`.
- `StateFamily<T>` ("holds one value of type `T` per `id`", "its reference must carry `id: string`"), read and written with `$.state.get` and `$.state.set` on `{ ...kept, id }` ("only a family member's `id` may be computed"; the value is "JSON data ... never `undefined`", hence `null` for an empty slot).
- `$.store.get/set`, `$.command.register`, `on('command.run', { command: 'seen-widget' })`, `on('session.start')`, the three `on('ui.render')` hooks with `e.surface` passed to `show`, `$.widgets.card`, `atom`/`read`/`update`. Not used: `$.model`, `$.http`, `prompt.submit`.
## State and storage
- `$.state` `isOn: boolean`.
- `$.state` `shots: SeenShot[]`, default `[]`, newest first, at most 4. `SeenShot`: `{ id: number; tool: string; source: string; type: 'PNG' | 'JPEG' | 'GIF' | 'WEBP'; bytes: number; width: number; height: number; at: number; isAgent: boolean; isKept: boolean; isPending: boolean; thumb: { width: number; height: number; rgb: string } | null }` (`width` and `height` 0 when unknown; `rgb` base64, 3 bytes a pixel).
- `$.state` `kept: StateFamily<{ id: number; media: string; data: string } | null>`, members `'0'` to `'3'` only, one picture each (at most about 4 000 100 characters of JSON); `null` or never written means no bytes. Emptying `kept` writes `null` to all four. While drawing, only the newest shot's slot is read, and only when an `Image` will be drawn; `open` reads the one slot it needs.
- `$.state` `canDraw: boolean`, default `false`: the pixel-terminal answer from the last capture. `$.state` `hasPage: boolean`, default `false`.
- `$.store` `isOn` only. File: the page. One module-level `let timer: Timer | undefined`; the generator lives in the closure of the timer's `step` and nowhere else. Nothing about a picture reaches the store.
## Off
No card; `tool.call` returns `next(e)` with no environment read and no state write; `session.end` does nothing. Switching off empties `shots` and `kept` and empties the page if this session wrote it. `sync` cancels the thumb timer, so no slice runs while off.
## Demo
`docs/engine.js` has no picture in its scripted turn, its `Read` answers text only, and its `env.get` answers only `HOME`, so the card draws blocks through the Raster that `docs/view.js` already has. Stand-in needed: one scripted call after the last `Bash`, `{ tool: 'Read', input: { file_path: ROOT + '/out/shot.png' }, ms: 500, image: '<base64 of a 96×54 PNG: white page, red bar across the top>' }`, answered as `{ result: { type: 'image', file: { base64, type: 'image/png', originalSize } }, text: '' }`. At rest: the empty sentence. After the turn: note `1 picture`, `#1 …/out/shot.png  96×54`, `Drawing the preview…` until the first tick of the engine's `clock.every` (54 rows are one slice), then the white page with its red bar in a 36 by 10 box, the detail row and the hint row. Without the stand-in the card stays empty and must not throw.
## Live
`bun factory/tools/live.ts factory/floor/plugins/seen-widget --allow "Bash,Read" --say "/seen-widget on" --say "Run exactly this with the Bash tool: echo iVBORw0KGgoAAAANSUhEUgAAAAEAAAABCAYAAAAfFcSJAAAADUlEQVR42mP8z8BQDwAEhQGAhKmMIQAAAABJRU5ErkJggg== | base64 -d > seen.png  Then Read seen.png with the Read tool and answer ok." --say "/seen-widget show" --say "/seen-widget off"`
One small turn. `open` is left out on purpose: it would start a browser on the owner's machine. A good run: `show` answers one line, `#1 Read <path>seen.png: PNG 1×1, 70 B, <HH:MM>, preview`, which proves the result shape, that `Read` kept the file a PNG, and that the timer job and the inflate run in the real module environment (`preview coming` here means the timer never fired, a fault); the store prints `isOn` false. The builder logs the type and size `Read` returned. `JPEG` or `no preview` here is a fault to report, not to work around. Before shipping the owner runs `open` once by hand; no agent has run it for real.
## Cost
No tokens, no model call, nothing added to context, nothing sent off the machine. Ceiling: a capture adds under 50 ms of the hook's own time to a tool call, whatever the picture and however many the call carries, because no pixel is decoded there. The thumb is drawn afterwards for the newest picture only, about 2.1 microseconds a pixel of the plugin's time in slices of about 35 ms with 5 ms between them: about 4.5 s of work for 1920×1080, about 9 s at the 4 200 000 pixel ceiling, none past it. The builder measures the capture and the longest slice for 1920×1080 in `claude plugin test`, not under bun, and logs them; a capture over 50 ms or a slice over 100 ms is a fault to report, not to work around. Up to 4 pictures of at most 3 MB each (4 000 000 base64 characters) are held in session state, each as its own value, 16 000 000 characters at the very most; no single value passes the 4 194 304 limit. One HTML file under the plugin's folder, written only by `open` and emptied by `clear`, `off` and the session's end.
## Acceptance
Fixtures are PNGs compressed by a real zlib and pasted as base64, plus one hand-made with stored blocks; `process.run`, `env.get` and `fs.write` are answered and recorded through `ground()`; thumbs appear only as the kit's clock is advanced. Every test that decodes more than 100 000 pixels takes the `SLOW` options, and `check.ts` must pass five runs in a row.
- A1: switched on with nothing seen, the card shows the empty sentence and no note in all three placements, and `show` and `open` answer `No pictures yet.`
- A2: a `Read` result of type `image` holding a 1280×720 PNG adds `#1` with the path, `PNG`, the size from the base64 length, 1280×720 from the header and the clock's time; the hook returns the very object `next` gave, and before the clock moves the shot is pending with no thumb and the card draws the head row, `Drawing the preview…`, the detail row and the hint row; after 59 ticks of 5 ms it is still pending, and the 60th writes the thumb and the card draws a Raster of 36 by 10 cells in that row's place.
- A3: fixtures of colour types 0, 2, 3, 4 and 6 at 8 bits, each of the five row filters, and stored, fixed and dynamic blocks decode to a thumb whose left half is one known colour and right half another, and a half-transparent pixel is laid over white. A 1920×1080 fixture is pending after 134 ticks and drawn after the 135th; a second picture captured mid-job takes over (the first is no longer pending and never gets a thumb, the second gets its own); and a fresh `session.start` with a pending newest shot in state draws its thumb from the kept bytes.
- A4: a JPEG, a GIF, a WebP, an interlaced PNG, a 16-bit PNG, a PNG header claiming 4 200 001 pixels and one claiming 4097 by 100 are never pending and start no timer, while a header claiming 2100 by 2000 is pending; a truncated PNG and a PNG with a corrupt stream are pending until their job fails; each ends with no thumb, draws `No preview for <TYPE> here.`, never throws and returns the result untouched, and `open` still writes its page.
- A5: an MCP result as `{ content: [{ type: 'image', data, mimeType }] }`, as a bare array of such blocks, and with `{ type: 'image', source: { media_type, data } }` blocks each adds a picture whose source is the short tool name; five image blocks in one call add the last 4, only the newest of them pending; a call with `agentId` shows ` · agent` on the card and ` (agent)` in `show`.
- A6: nothing is captured and no state is written for a `Read` of text, a `Bash` call, an MCP result of text blocks only, a block of media type `image/svg+xml`, a result with `isError`, a `deny`, and a result that is `null` or a string. A `Read` picture whose call has no `file_path` is listed as `#<n> (no path)` and the card still draws; a source of `a`, a line break, `b.png` reads `a b.png` on the card and in `show`.
- A7: four pictures of 1 500 000 base64 characters each are all kept, in slots `1`, `2`, `3` and `0`, and `open 1` to `open 4` each write their own data; the fifth picture drops `#1` from `shots` and takes its slot in `kept`, the note reads `5 pictures`, and the card draws `#5` in full with `#4`, `#3`, `#2` as one row each with their types.
- A8: the box is 36 by 10 at 40 columns and 16 by 5 at 20 for 1280×720, 43 by 12 at 60, and 11 by 12 at 40 for 900×1950; the Raster is exactly that many cells and a thumb that is red above and white below draws red cells above white ones.

- A10: `open` writes the page with `data:image/jpeg;base64,<data>` for the newest and `open 2` for `#2`, a source of `a<b>&".png` is escaped in the title, and the viewer is `rundll32 url.dll,FileProtocolHandler <page>` when `OS` is `Windows_NT`, `open <page>` otherwise, and `xdg-open <page>` when `open` rejects or exits 1; the answer is `Opened #<n>: <page>`; the page is `C:\p\seen-widget\seen.html` for a root of `C:\p\seen-widget` and `/p/seen-widget/seen.html` for `/p/seen-widget`.

The card holds #<a> to #<b>.`; a picture of 4 000 000 base64 characters is kept and `open` writes it whole; one of 4 000 001 is listed with `Not kept: too large.`, `open` answers `#<n> was too large to keep (<size>).` and writes nothing, also when it is `#5` and `#1` was kept in the same slot; `open x`, `open 0`, `open 1.5` and `open 99999999999999999999` answer the usage; no capture alone ever calls `fs.write` or `process.run`.
