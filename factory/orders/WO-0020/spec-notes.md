# WO-0020 Loupe: spec notes

The spec passes. Every call it names is in the types file (`plugin-authoring/types/claude-code.d.ts`, build 2.1.289): `$.ui.selection` with `UiSelection { text, requestId? }`, `$.ui.copy` with `UiCopyArgs` and `UiCopyResult`, `Button` `onPress` with `press.surface`, `$.process.run` with `cwd`, `timeoutMs`, `exitCode`, `stdout`, `isStdoutTruncated`, `$.fs.exists`, `$.fs.stat` with `{ resolve: true }` and `realPath`, `$.session.root`, `cwd`, `repo`, `$.clock.every`, `$.clock.now`, `$.ui.toast`, `tool.call` with `tool` and `tool_use_id`, `viewport?.isFullscreen` and `presentation.isFullscreen`. The drawings fit (36 inner at 40 columns, 16 at 20; the middle cut on the button row is exact). 114 lines, 15 acceptance lines. Settle these while building; each will be looked at on the bench.

1. **Call budget.** Data says at most 2 fs calls per examine, but a path found only under the cwd takes `exists` (root), `exists` (cwd) and `stat`: three. Keep the behaviour of Kinds and A4; the budget is 3 fs calls. Say so in Cost wording in `widget.json` only if it mentions a number.
2. **Row cap with a colour.** "At most 6 rows", but a colour with a source is head, swatch (2 rows high), rgb, source, button: 7. Either the swatch counts as one row of the cap or the cap is 7 for colour; the card must not drop the button or the source to fit.
3. **`realPath` may be absent.** The types say `realPath` is absent when the path leads nowhere or a hook above withheld it. Absent must be treated as outside the root (the one row, no git, no copy), never as inside.
4. **A found name without a slash is a path.** `look scratch` in the Live line becomes `path` if a file or folder named `scratch` sits in the root, and `Copied` then names a path. Likewise a symbol with a definition copies `path:line`, not the name. The live answers listed are examples; judge the run by the kind rules, not by the literal `Copied scratch.`
5. **Plurals.** `1 mention in 1 file`, `1 file` on a commit, `1 character, 1 line`, and the tight `1× in 1 file` go through `plural()`. `(+1)` for two definitions.
6. **Spans.** Every age goes through `span()`; a commit or file dated in the future (clock skew) must not print a negative span or `-3s ago`. `time` at exactly now reads `0s ago`, not `in 0s`.
7. **The 13-digit range.** The range 2001-09-09 to now plus ten years applies to the value in milliseconds for 13 digits; `0999999999999` is not a time. A 10-digit number that is also hex is tried as a commit first, by the stated order; that costs one `rev-parse` and is fine.
8. **Definition match on long lines.** The definition line's text is trimmed and truncated at the end; a minified line of many kilobytes must not be stored whole in `rows` (cut it to a sane length, say 200 characters, before it reaches state). The grep answer is parsed as `path:line:text` with the first two colons only, so a Windows-style or colon-holding text does not shift the fields.
9. **`grep` scope.** `git grep` runs from the session root, which may be a subfolder of the repository; the `path:line` shown and copied should be one the path kind would then find (relative to the root).
10. **Typed finding outside fullscreen.** With `viewport.isFullscreen` false and a `typed` finding, the card shows the finding, not the fullscreen sentence. With `viewport` absent (not `false`) the ordinary rest sentence is drawn.
11. **Bare `look` and `seen`.** Only a poll writes `seen`, so after a bare `look` the next poll sees the same selection as new and examines it once more. Harmless, but it must not flicker the card to `looking…` and back for an identical finding; reuse the finding when text and `requestId` match the one on the card.
12. **Poll that rejects.** `ui.selection` rejecting on every tick must not toast, log per tick or leave `isBusy` set. Same for a poll overlapping a `look` verb: the ticket rule decides, and `isBusy` is cleared by whichever examine holds the current ticket, including on failure.
13. **Source name.** `mcp__a__b` shows `b`; a server or tool name that itself holds `__` takes the part after the last `mcp__<server>__` split, as A10's `run_query_` example implies. The cut to 10 characters is a plain cut, no `…`.
14. **One-line answer.** The `look` answer for `text` reads `<head>: text · 31 characters, 2 lines`; a multi-line or 200+ character head is cut in the middle to one line there too, so the command never answers with a wall of the selection.
15. **Copy toast length.** `Copied <value>` with a 40-character hash or a long path is fine in a toast, but cut the value in the middle past about 60 characters.
16. **Non-terminal surface.** `$.widgets.picture` answers a dim sentence off the terminal; on those surfaces the colour card should still show the `rgb(...)` row and the button, and the swatch sentence must not be cut mid-word at 20 columns.
