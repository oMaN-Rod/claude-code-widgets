# WO-0018 Tap: spec notes

The spec passes. Every call it names is in the types file (`plugin-authoring/types/claude-code.d.ts`, build 2.1.289): `$.mcp.call` with `McpToolResult` and `McpContentBlock`, `$.tool.list` with `ToolInfo`, `$.clock.every` (`TimerCall`, a `Timer` with `cancel()`), `$.clock.now`, `$.ui.toast`. The drawings fit their widths (16 inner at 20 columns, 36 at 40). 107 lines, 15 acceptance lines. Settle these while building; each will be looked at on the bench.

1. `$.clock.now` is `() => Promise<number>`. Await it everywhere; a bare `$.clock.now()` in arithmetic gives `NaN` ages.
2. `now` is stored only by the tick, but `readAt` and `askedAt` are set when a command or a call runs. Right after `add` or `run`, `now` is older than `readAt`, and the age stays `0s` for up to a minute, then jumps. Store `now` whenever a call is asked or settles, so the age is true from the first frame.
3. Overlapping ticks. A tick awaits its calls one after the other; the next tick fires 60 s later whether or not the first has finished. With tap 1 hanging, the second tick would call tap 2 while tap 1 is unsettled, against A9 ("the second only after the first settles"), and after 10 minutes a hung tap is due again and would be asked twice at once, against the Terms. A tick must do nothing while an earlier tick is still calling. Keep that marker in `$.state` (the timer handle is the only module-level `let`), and reset it when switching off. Say in the report what happens when a call never settles: the types promise no timeout for `$.mcp.call`.
4. The first minute. After `session.start` or switching on, every tap sits on `not read yet` until the first tick 60 s later. That matches A1; keep it, but check on the render that the Working card does not look broken, and that `run <n>` during that minute works.
5. `raw` and cleaning. "Runs of spaces become one" and "trimmed" flatten `JSON.stringify(value, null, 1)` below the first level, while A4 asks for "indented JSON with its line breaks". For `raw`, keep leading spaces of each line (collapse nothing, or only inside control-character replacements), and trim only the whole text.
6. `1 events`, `1 issues`. The object rule prints `<n> <key>` with the server's own key, so one item reads `1 events: Standup`. A plural that is wrong on the card is a finding at inspection. Drop one trailing `s` from the key when `n` is 1 and the key ends in a single `s` (not `ss`), or settle another rule and test it.
7. `a image block` (Terms and A3) should read `an image block`, `an audio block`; `a text block`, `a resource block`. Choose the article by the first letter and fix the A3 test to match.
8. An array whose first item has no label but later ones do: the rule looks at the first item only. Fine; keep it and do not scan.
9. `list a b` (two words) is not specified. Take everything after `list` as the word, trimmed, so `No MCP tool matches a b.` Also clean `word` before it is echoed in the answer, and cut it to a sane length (40).
10. `add` echoes: `<tool>` in the read-only sentence and the parser's message in the JSON sentence are typed text; clean them and cut the tool to 60 characters as the toast does.
11. `run`, `show`, `drop` with `1.5`, `-1`, `01` or `1 extra`: "a whole number" must mean digits only and nothing after; `0` and numbers past the count answer `No tap <n>.`, the others the usage line. Test at least `1.5` and `1 x`.
12. Restoring `taps` from the store: treat it as untrusted. Keep only entries with string `server` and `tool` and a plain-object `args`, at most 4; anything else is dropped without an error. A store written by hand must not crash `session.start`.
13. Faults in the 20-column frame: `server error:…` tells nothing past the prefix. That is what the spec draws, so it stands, but `run <n>` and the toastless card are then the only way to read it: make sure `run <n>` answers the whole fault (100 characters).
14. A fault row's age uses `askedAt`; while a call to an already-read tap is in flight, the row still shows the old reading and its `readAt` age. Do not flash `…` or `not read yet` on a tap that has a reading.
15. Demo. The spec admits the change comes ten minutes in. A visitor can see it at once with `/tap-widget run 2` (the stand-in's second call answers the third issue). If the demo page can carry a hint or a third opening line, use `run 2` so the page shows `* 3 issues: Null user in session.js`; if it cannot, say so in the report.
16. Toast text: the reading cut to 60 gets `…` when cut, as on the card.
17. Two sessions: `taps` is read from the store once per session, so an `add` in one session is not seen by another until it restarts, and a `drop` there can be overwritten by a later `add` here. This follows from cutting the shared file; leave it, and do not add a file.
